01

The idea

Where we live, booking a haircut still means messages and phone calls. Amer and I wanted to get rid of that: clients book themselves, any barber in the shop, at any free time.

who-with.png
02

Three taps for clients

Barber, service, time. Clients find their shop with its code or a QR, swipe to their barber, and every choice fills in the title until the booking reads as one sentence.

barber-side.png
03

The barber’s side

Each barber has their own schedule, and the day sits on one screen: appointments, breaks, no-shows and earnings. A client who keeps not showing up can’t book in the app anymore.

free-times.png
04

Built together

We both did design, front-end and back-end, taking turns so one could rest while the other worked. React Native and Expo, with Supabase behind it. The time wheel only offers slots where the whole service fits.

early-access.png
05

Tested, then early access

Before Google Play accepted the Android app, 12 testers used it in a closed test. The iPhone version is in App Store review now, and early access is free.

What you're seeing

  1. Crosshair: a pixel “+” in place of the arrow. It inverts against whatever is behind it, so it's visible on light and dark.
  2. Selection frame: hover a button, link or card and four pixel corners snap around it, like Minecraft's block outline.
  3. Block-break particles: click and a few dirt and grass pixels pop out and fall.
  4. Hold to break: hold the mouse on a button or card. Cracks spread, pixels chip off, and after a moment it breaks with a big burst. Let go early and the cracks vanish.
